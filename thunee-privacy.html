Thunee Royale Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · applies to the Thunee Royale app for Android (com.thuneeroyale.app)

The short version

Thunee Royale does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (deck style, felt colour, speed, sound, your chosen name) and your record (matches, wins, Thunees, Royals, whitewashes) in the app's own storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Internet use

The game plays fully offline. When your phone is online, the app downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google's servers receive your device's IP address to deliver the fonts. We do not receive or store this information. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that need data, such as online play, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's Play Store data safety section will be updated to match.
