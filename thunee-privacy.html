Thunee Royale Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 (updated for v1.1 online play) · applies to the Thunee Royale app for Android, iPhone and iPad (com.thuneeroyale.app)

The short version

Single player, Pass & play, Live table and local tournaments keep everything on your phone. Only if you use Online rooms or share a tournament online does the app store game data on Google Firebase, under an anonymous ID with no email, phone number or sign-in. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

Online rooms and shared tournaments

To run an online game or a shared tournament, the app uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Anonymous Authentication). Firebase gives your copy of the app a random anonymous ID. The following is stored:

  • Online rooms: the 4-letter room code, the name you type in, your anonymous ID, whether you are connected, and the cards and calls of the game. Each player's phone only receives their own cards.
  • Shared tournaments: the tournament name, the player and team names you enter, fixtures and results. Anyone with the tournament code can see and update it.

This data is used only to run the game between the phones in the room or tournament. It is stored on Google's servers in Belgium (europe-west1). Anonymous IDs are deleted automatically after 30 days. You can ask for room or tournament data to be deleted using the contact below. See Firebase privacy and security for how Google handles it.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (deck style, felt colour, speed, sound, your chosen name) and your record (matches, wins, Thunees, Royals, whitewashes) in the app's own storage on your phone. Apart from the name you use in an online room, this information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Internet use

The game plays offline except for online rooms and shared tournaments (above). When your phone is online, the app also downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google's servers receive your device's IP address to deliver the fonts. We do not receive or store this information. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Online rooms only store the name a player chooses to type in.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is used, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's Play Store data safety section will be updated to match.
