Thunee Royale

The Durban card game · iPhone, iPad and Android

Six cards, one hidden trump and a partner across the felt. Play against the computer, pass one phone around, play online with friends on their own phones, or use the Live table and Tournament tools to score your real card nights.

Getting started

  • Play → choose Single player, Pass & play or Online → Deal me in.
  • How to play on the home screen has the full illustrated rules: calling, Jodi and Marials, Thunee and Royals, Double, Khanaak, 4-balls and Give Win.
  • Turn on Coach me to have every card graded, and open the Partner Lab to program how your computer partner plays.

Online rooms

One player creates a room and shares the 4-letter code or QR. Friends tap Play → Online → Join. Empty seats are played by the computer. Only the name each player types is stored, under an anonymous ID.
