Common questions

My prayer times differ from my mosque

Open Settings → Location & prayer times and choose the calculation method used in your country, check your Asr madhab (Shafi'i/majority or Hanafi) under Profile & madhab, then fine-tune each prayer under Mosque adjustments. Always follow your local mosque where it differs.

I don't hear the azan on iPhone

Allow notifications for Noor Pro, and in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Noor Pro turn on Time Sensitive Notifications. iPhone limits alert sounds to 30 seconds; tap the alert to hear the full azan in the app. Silent mode and Focus can mute alerts.

Can I use my own azan recording?

Yes. Open the azan voices screen, tap Choose an audio file and pick a recording from your phone (MP3 or M4A works best). It is set for all prayers, and you can change each prayer under Settings → Azan & alerts. On iPhone, alerts play the first 30 seconds; tap the alert to hear it in full. Please only use recordings you have permission to use.

The Qibla compass is not steady

Move away from metal objects and magnets, then move the phone in a figure-of-eight to calibrate.

How do Family Khatam groups work?

Create a group, tap Invite → Turn on live sync and share the code. Members join with Join / Sync Group and the code; claims and completed Juz update for everyone. See the privacy policy for what is stored.

How do I change a widget's colour?

iPhone/iPad: touch and hold the widget → Edit Widget → choose a colour and shade. Android: touch and hold → Edit widget (or the colour screen when you add it).
