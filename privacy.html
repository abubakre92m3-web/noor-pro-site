Noor Pro · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Applies to Noor Pro for iPhone, iPad and Android · Effective 18 October 2026

In short: Noor Pro has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your prayer log, settings, counters and location stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device is what you choose to share in an online Family Khatam group, and the normal web requests needed to stream Quran text and audio.

1. Who we are

Noor Pro ("the app", "we") is an independent Islamic prayer and Quran companion published by Abubakr Ebrahim. For any privacy question, email abubakre92m3@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Location. If you allow it, the app uses your location to calculate prayer times, sunrise and sunset, to point the Qibla compass and to notice when you are travelling (Qasr). All calculations run on your device. Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your records and settings. Prayer log, missed (qadha) prayers, fasting records, tasbeeh counts, Quran reading and memorisation progress, Umrah and wudhu status, Zakat and inheritance calculator entries, theme and alert choices. These are saved in the app's private storage on your device (on iPhone, shared only with the app's own Home Screen widgets).
  • Notifications. Azan and reminder alerts are scheduled on your device. No push server is used.
  • Motion and compass sensors. Used only to turn the Qibla compass. Readings are not stored.
  • Arabic speech. The "Listen" buttons use your device's built-in text-to-speech voice.

3. Microphone (Hifz practice, optional)

If you turn on recitation practice, the app asks for microphone access to follow your recitation. Noor Pro does not record, save or upload audio. On some devices the app uses the speech recognition built into the operating system. When it does, your recitation may be processed by Apple (iPhone, iPad) or Google (Android) under their own privacy policies, in the same way as dictation. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device settings.

4. Family Khatam groups (optional, only if you put a group online)

If you choose Turn on live sync or join a group with a code, the following is stored on Google Firebase (Realtime Database, hosted in Belgium, EU) so that everyone in the group sees the same board:

  • the group code and group title;
  • the names, roles and avatar emoji you or other members enter for the group;
  • which Juz each member has claimed or completed;
  • a random anonymous ID created for your copy of the app (no email, phone number or account is involved).

Anyone who has the group code can see and update that group, so only share the code with people you trust, and use first names or nicknames if you prefer. Groups cannot be listed or searched. To have an online group deleted, email us with its code. Group data is otherwise kept while the group is in use.

5. Content the app loads from the internet

To keep the app small, some content is streamed when you use it. Your device contacts these services directly, and like any website they receive your IP address and basic request details. No personal information from the app is sent to them.

  • AlQuran Cloud and Islamic Network: Quran text, translations and recitation audio;
  • EveryAyah.com: recitation audio;
  • GitHub, jsDelivr and Wikimedia Commons: azan sound files;
  • Google Firebase: only for online Family Khatam groups (see section 4).

Links to other websites (for example the UAE Awqaf website, or sharing through WhatsApp) open in your browser or the other app, under that service's own privacy policy.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of any data.
  • No user accounts or sign-in.

7. Deleting your information

Everything stored on your device is removed when you delete the app (iPhone and iPad) or clear its storage or uninstall it (Android). For an online Family Khatam group, email us the group code and we will delete it.

8. Children

Noor Pro is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child takes part in a family group, a parent can ask us to delete that group at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
