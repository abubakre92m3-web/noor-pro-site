Rummy Royale Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · applies to the Rummy Royale app for Android, iPhone and iPad (com.rummyroyale.app)

The short version

Games against the computer keep everything on your phone. Only if you play Online, start an open game, or make a player profile for Friends does the app store data on Google Firebase, under an anonymous ID with no email, phone number or sign-in. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

Online rooms

To run an online game, the app uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Anonymous Authentication). Firebase gives your copy of the app a random anonymous ID. For each room the following is stored: the room code, the name you type in, your anonymous ID, whether you are connected, the game settings, and the cards and moves of the game. Each player's phone only receives their own cards.

This data is used only to run the game between the phones in the room. It is stored on Google's servers in Belgium (europe-west1). Anonymous IDs are deleted automatically after 30 days. You can ask for room data to be deleted using the contact below. See Firebase privacy and security for how Google handles it.

Open games

A player can start an open game that anyone online in Rummy Royale may join without a code. While it is open, everyone signed in to the app can see the host's name, the number of seats and how many are free, and whether the game is waiting, calling for players or already playing. When the host starts, other players with the app open get a notice with a Join button. The listing is removed when the room closes or the host's phone goes offline.

Player profile and friends

Friends is optional. If you make a profile, the app stores your name (or just your initials, if you choose), the face you pick and a player code. Anyone signed in to the app can see these three things, so people can add you by code or QR. There is no name search and no chat.

Your friends list, friend requests and table invites are stored so they reach the right phone. Only your friends can see whether you have the app open or are in a game, and your record (games played and won, rounds, times you went out). You can block someone, remove a friend, or delete your profile, friends and invites at any time under Online → Friends.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings (players, names, card design, table, speed, sound) and your record (games played and won, rounds, times you went out) in the app's own storage on your phone. Apart from the name you use in an online room, this information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Internet use

The game plays offline except for online rooms (above). When your phone is online, the app also downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts, Firebase's code from Google when you open Online play, and a small QR-code library from cdnjs (Cloudflare) when you host a room. As with any web request, those servers receive your device's IP address to deliver the files. We do not receive or store this information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Online rooms only store the name a player chooses to type in. There is no real-money play, betting or prizes.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is used, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app's store privacy details will be updated to match.
